# Florida BDI election limit: five vs. eight — settled

**Question:** Is the lifetime limit on Basic Driver Improvement (BDI) traffic-school elections under Florida Statutes § 318.14(9) five or eight?

**VERDICT: EIGHT.** The statute says eight. The July 1, 2024 amendment *raised* the limit from five to eight. FLHSMV's website pages saying five are stale — they still carry the pre-amendment number, even though FLHSMV's own Driving Records FAQ cites the July 1, 2024 effective date of the very amendment that raised it.

Verified live November 23, 2026. All quotes below are verbatim from primary sources.

---

## 1. Current § 318.14(9) — verbatim

**Source:** Online Sunshine (official site of the Florida Legislature), § 318.14, opened live 2026-11-23 (page crawled 2026-11-23 21:16 UTC).
**URL:** https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&Search_String=479.261&URL=0300-0399/0318/Sections/0318.14.html

> "(9) Any person who does not hold a commercial driver license or commercial learner's permit and who is cited while driving a noncommercial motor vehicle for an infraction under this section other than a violation of s. 316.183(2), s. 316.187, or s. 316.189 when the driver exceeds the posted limit by 30 miles per hour or more, s. 320.0605, s. 320.07(3)(a) or (b), s. 322.065, s. 322.15(1), s. 322.61, or s. 322.62 may, in lieu of a court appearance, elect to attend in the location of his or her choice within this state a basic driver improvement course approved by the Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles. In such a case, adjudication must be withheld, any civil penalty that is imposed by s. 318.18(3) must be reduced by 18 percent, and points, as provided by s. 322.27, may not be assessed. However, a person may not make an election under this subsection if the person has made an election under this subsection in the preceding 12 months. **A person may not make more than eight elections within his or her lifetime under this subsection.** The requirement for community service under s. 318.18(8) is not waived by a plea of nolo contendere or by the withholding of adjudication of guilt by a court."

**Corroboration — 2025 Florida Statutes:** The Florida Senate's 2025 edition of § 318.14(9), opened live 2026-11-23 (page crawled 2026-06-13), contains the identical sentence: "A person may not make more than eight elections within his or her lifetime under this subsection."
**URL:** https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/0318.14

**History line (same pages):** the statute's history ends "...; s. 3, ch. 2023-171; s. 12, ch. 2023-174; **s. 3, ch. 2024-173**; s. 1, ch. 2024-192; s. 2, ch. 2025-77." — i.e., § 3 of Chapter 2024-173 is the amendment that put "eight" in the text.

## 2. The pre-amendment text — "five" (2023 Florida Statutes)

**Source:** 2023 Florida Statutes, Chapter 318, Florida Senate site. **Flag: search-indexed copy, NOT a live open** — a live open of this page returned HTTP 429 (rate limit) and was not retried. Search-indexed copy of https://www.FLSenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2023/Chapter318/All (crawled ~359 days before 2026-11-23). The pre-amendment wording is independently confirmed by the session law's strikethrough text in § 3 below, so this does not rest on the indexed copy alone.

> "(9) ... However, a person may not make an election under this subsection if the person has made an election under this subsection in the preceding 12 months. **A person may make no more than five elections within his or her lifetime under this subsection.** ..."

So "five" was the law **before** July 1, 2024 — not after.

## 3. The session law that changed it — Chapter 2024-173, Laws of Florida

**Citation:** Chapter 2024-173, Laws of Florida (Committee Substitute for Committee Substitute for Committee Substitute for House Bill No. 287 — CS/CS/HB 287, 2024), Section 3.
**Source:** Official session-law PDF, opened live 2026-11-23 (crawled 2026-11-23 21:16 UTC).
**URL:** http://laws.flrules.org/files/Ch_2024-173.pdf

**Title/preamble (states the direction of the change):**

> "amending s. 318.14, F.S.; **increasing the number of times a driver may elect to attend a basic driver improvement course** approved by the Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles in lieu of a court appearance; ..."

**Section 3 (amending § 318.14(9)):**

> "Section 3. Subsection (9) of section 318.14, Florida Statutes, is amended to read: ... A person may not make more than **eight five** elections within his or her lifetime under this subsection."

The PDF's own coding note reads: "CODING: Words stricken are deletions; words underlined are additions." In the enrolled text, **"eight" is the underlined addition and "five" is the stricken deletion** — i.e., the amendment struck "five" and inserted "eight." (The bill-text markup in the companion bill SB 266 renders this unambiguously as "more than ==eight== ~~five~~ elections.")

**Effective date — Section 16 of the same act:**

> "Section 16. This act shall take effect **July 1, 2024**."

**Direction of the change, confirmed:** **5 → 8, effective July 1, 2024.** The claim that the July 1, 2024 amendment cut the limit from 8 to 5 is backwards. The bill analyses for both HB 287 and SB 266 state the same thing in plain language ("Increases from five to eight the number of ... Basic Driver Improvement course elections that are allowed in a lifetime"; "The bill has an effective date of July 1, 2024").

## 5. Why the FLHSMV pages are wrong / stale

1. **The statute is the law, and it says eight.** Both the current Online Sunshine text and the 2025 Florida Statutes say "more than eight elections within his or her lifetime." A state agency's web guidance cannot override the statute.
2. **The amendment went 5 → 8, not 8 → 5.** Chapter 2024-173, § 3 struck "five" and inserted "eight," its title says it is "increasing the number of times," and § 16 made it effective July 1, 2024. Any worker claiming the July 1, 2024 amendment cut the limit from 8 to 5 has the direction backwards.
3. **FLHSMV's FAQ is internally self-contradictory, which exposes the error.** It attributes "five" to § 318.14(9) "**Effective July 1, 2024**" — but July 1, 2024 is the effective date of the amendment that *raised* the limit to eight. The FAQ's date points at the very change that falsifies its number: the page was evidently annotated with the amendment's effective date while the old "five" was never updated. The Driver Improvement Schools page still states the pre-July 1, 2024 rule verbatim.
4. **Why the earlier "8" reading was right.** The earlier sweep that read the 2024 Florida Statutes on flhouse.gov and found "eight" was correct: the 2024 published statutes already reflected the July 1, 2024 amendment. There was never a version of the current statute saying five — the "five" lives only in FLHSMV's un-updated web pages (and in pre-July 2024 sources quoting the old law).
5. **Nothing else changed.** The 12-month bar ("may not make an election ... if the person has made an election ... in the preceding 12 months") is unchanged, and the separate 3-election lifetime limit in § 318.14(10) (nolo contendere / proof-of-compliance elections) is a different subsection and was not changed by this amendment — do not confuse the two.

**Practical caution (implication, not a separate verified fact):** because FLHSMV — the agency that keeps the driving record — still publishes "five" on its own pages, a driver counting on a 6th, 7th, or 8th election should confirm with the clerk of court in the county where the citation was issued before relying on it. The statute controls, but the agency's stale guidance is what drivers, and some clerks, will see.

---

## 6. One-paragraph summary (for the reference file)

Florida's lifetime limit on Basic Driver Improvement traffic-school elections is **eight, not five**. Section 318.14(9), Florida Statutes, now reads: "A person may not make more than eight elections within his or her lifetime under this subsection." The limit was five until Chapter 2024-173, Laws of Florida (CS/CS/HB 287), § 3, struck "five" and inserted "eight" — an increase the act's own title describes — effective July 1, 2024 (§ 16). FLHSMV's Driver Improvement Schools page and its Driving Records FAQ still say "five," but they are stale: the FAQ even attributes "five" to § 318.14(9) "Effective July 1, 2024," which is the effective date of the amendment that raised the limit to eight. The statute governs; the FLHSMV pages were never updated after the amendment.
